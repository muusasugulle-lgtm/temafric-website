About us

A commodity trading group built for African industry.

From our headquarters in Lubumbashi and offices in five other countries, we close the supply gap for energy and essential industrial goods.

Who we are

Welcome to Temafric.

With roots in Dubai, UAE, where the group began in 2004 as Anbarland General Trading, Temafric is a commodity trading company focused on closing the supply gap for energy and essential industrial goods on the African continent. Since 2024 our headquarters have been in the DRC, from where we keep mines, contractors, transporters and manufacturers running with dependable deliveries of fuel, lubricants, steel, bitumen and mining consumables.

Our edge comes from a practical, on-the-ground approach: long-standing relationships with refineries and manufacturers, a disciplined logistics chain that crosses borders every day, and a close understanding of the markets we serve. Our group companies, Somafric Investment in Zambia, MAMS Trading in Ethiopia, Borderline in Tanzania and Anbarland General Trading in Dubai, together with our Djibouti office, put us close to both our customers and the ports and trading hubs that supply them. Every partnership we build is aimed at one thing: making supply predictable for our customers.

Mission

To deliver reliable, high-quality energy and industrial supplies that keep African industry productive: safely, on time and at fair value.

Vision

To be Africa’s most trusted supply partner for fuel, lubricants and commodities, the name industry calls first when supply matters most.

Why Temafric

Our core strengths

  • Security of supply — term agreements with refineries, blenders and manufacturers keep product flowing when markets tighten.
  • Own storage and fleet — depots and tankers under our control, so delivery times are ours to keep.
  • Quality assured — product testing at loading and delivery, with full certificates of quality and traceability.
  • Safety and compliance — HSE systems built to mining-industry standards, and full regulatory licensing in every market.
  • Local knowledge, global reach — teams on the ground who know the borders, the regulations and the customers.
