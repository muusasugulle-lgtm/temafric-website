About us

Welcome to Temafric.

With roots in Dubai, UAE, where the group began in 2004 as Anbarland General Trading, Temafric is a commodity trading company focused on closing the supply gap for energy and essential industrial goods on the African continent. Since 2024 our headquarters have been in the DRC, from where we keep mines, contractors, transporters and manufacturers running with dependable deliveries of fuel, lubricants, steel, bitumen and mining consumables.

Our divisions

Five divisions. One supply chain.

Each division is specialised, but they work as one, so a single partner can cover a site’s fuel, lubrication, consumables and transport.

01DRC · Zambia · Ethiopia

Petroleum

Bulk and packed supply of diesel (AGO), petrol (PMS) and heavy fuel oil to mine sites, power plants, fleets and distributors, backed by depot storage and on-site fuel management.

02DRC · Zambia · Ethiopia

Lubricants

Engine, hydraulic, transmission and gear oils, greases and coolants for heavy mining equipment and commercial fleets, with oil analysis and lubrication programmes that extend equipment life.

03DRC · Zambia · Ethiopia

Steel & Bitumen

Trading and supply of construction and industrial steel, and bitumen for road building, sourced through Anbarland General Trading in Dubai and delivered to contractors, mines and infrastructure projects.

04DRC · Zambia

Mining Supply

Procurement and delivery of the goods mines consume daily: process reagents, grinding media, explosives accessories, OTR tyres, spare parts, PPE and site consumables.

05Cross-border corridors

Logistics

A tanker and haulage fleet with port handling, customs clearing and border services, run through Borderline in Dar es Salaam and our Djibouti office and delivering door to site.
